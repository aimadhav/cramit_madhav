Account controls

Delete your Cramit account.

The fastest method is inside the app: open Stats → Settings → Delete account permanently. You will be asked to confirm twice.

Can’t access the app?

Email us from the address registered to your Cramit account. Include only your account email—never send your password, access token or Google credentials.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted

  • Your authentication account and Cramit profile.
  • Synced review history, progress, due dates, bookmarks and notes.
  • Study sessions, active chapters and class memberships.
  • Classes owned by a non-admin teacher account.

What happens next

We will verify that the request comes from the registered address. Email requests are normally completed within 30 days. Some encrypted backups and security records may remain temporarily until their normal expiry. Content-administrator accounts or accounts owning shared decks require a manual transfer before deletion so other students do not lose published study material.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete a cloud account. Guest-mode data is local and can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
