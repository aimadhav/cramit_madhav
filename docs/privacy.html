Privacy, without the fine-print fog.

Cramit stores the minimum information needed to keep your revision progress available across devices and to show useful study analytics.

Information we process

  • Account information such as your user ID, email address, name and preparation focus.
  • Study activity such as reviews, ratings, response time, due dates, streaks, bookmarks, notes and active chapters.
  • Class membership and performance summaries when you voluntarily join a teacher’s class using its code.
  • Technical crash and performance information through Sentry, including app version, device model and error stack traces. Cramit does not intentionally send your email, authentication token, card answers or notes to Sentry.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, synchronize progress, schedule spaced-repetition reviews, calculate your statistics, restore your data on another device, operate joined classes, prevent abuse and diagnose failures.

Where data is stored

Cloud account and study data is processed using Supabase. Crash reports are processed using Sentry. Downloaded decks and offline progress may also remain locally on your device. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Teachers and classes

When you enter a class code, the teacher who owns that class can view your name and study-performance summaries. Teachers cannot use Cramit to access your password or authentication token. Leaving or deleting your account removes your active membership.

Guest mode

Guest-mode progress is stored only on that device. It is not uploaded and does not automatically transfer when you later create an account.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while your account is active. You can permanently delete a normal student account from Account & privacy inside the app. You can also request deletion using our account deletion page. Backups and security logs may take a limited period to expire.

Children

Cramit is an education tool and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can remove it.

Your choices

You may use guest mode without a cloud account, leave a joined class, sign out, request support, or delete your account. You can contact us about access or correction of your information.
